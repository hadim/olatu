# 0017 — Mobile layout, chart controls & the readout

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Supersedes / relates:** continues [0015 — Current Conditions density](2026-08-07-0015-current-conditions-density.md)
and [0016 — Touch charts](2026-08-07-0016-touch-charts.md) on the same surface, from the same
place: the owner on a phone. Alignment rules from 0015 §2 are **revised for narrow widths only**
(see §3); the desktop layout is unchanged.

## 1. Why

Five owner reports from a phone, plus what they exposed around them:

1. **The page wasted its width.** Three nested paddings stacked up: the page shell (`px-5`), the
   Current Conditions card (`px-5`) and each realm zone (`p-3.5`) — ~72 px of the 390 px viewport,
   ~18 %, gone before a single value. The chart host added its own `px-4` on top.
2. **The real-time values were not centred.** With the width above lost, the Sea tiles fell to
   *one* column (`max-[420px]:grid-cols-1`) and sat hard-left under a **centred** dial — a ragged
   left column with an empty right half. Same for the tide strip.
3. **The chart controls ate the screen.** Twelve range chips at a 44 px touch height wrapped to
   three rows; smoothing, the navigator and "jump to date" added three more. ~260 px of buttons
   above ~1700 px of charts, before any data.
4. **The hover readout was unscannable.** Icon · label · value ran inline, so a wrapped label
   ("Wave height" over two lines) pushed its value out of line with its neighbours' and nothing
   lined up column-to-column. Sea and Air readings were one undifferentiated run of chips, which
   is exactly where "which temperature is this?" bites.
5. **Hovering felt desynced between panels.** It was: `setCursor` only refreshed the readout card
   from **buoy** panels (`if (!(panel.tide || air))`). Hover the wind, tide, humidity or pressure
   panel and every crosshair moved while the card kept showing the *previous* panel's instant.

## 2. Decision — width

One rule: **only the page shell pays for the screen edge on a phone.** Inner containers keep their
identity (border + tint) but shrink their padding at narrow widths.

| Surface | ≤ 640 px | > 640 px |
|---|---|---|
| page shell | `px-3` | `px-5` |
| Current Conditions card | `px-3` | `px-5` |
| realm zone | `p-2.5` | `p-3.5` |
| chart host | `--ts-pad: 0.7rem` | `--ts-pad: 1rem` |

The chart host's padding becomes the **`--ts-pad` custom property** rather than a Tailwind class,
because three other things must track it exactly: the day-separator overlay (inset to line up with
`valToPos`), the drop-indicator line, and the `.ts-band` drag gutter (which lives in negative
margin *inside* that padding). They now all read the one variable — change it in one place.

Net: ~48 px back on a 390 px phone, ~12 % more plot width.

## 3. Decision — centring (narrow only)

0015 §2 chose left-aligned tiles at every width. That was right for the two/four-up desktop grid
and wrong under a **centred** dial in a stacked phone layout, which is the only place the zone
switches to one column above and tiles below.

- Below 720 px (where `ZONE_GRID` already stacks and centres the dial), tiles centre their content
  (`justify-items-center` + `text-center`), so the zone reads as one centred column.
- The Sea grid **keeps two columns down to the narrowest phone** — the one-column rule is dropped.
  Two columns of centred tiles is what makes the block symmetric; one column is what made it read
  as mis-set. The extra width from §2 pays for it.
- The tide strip centres its groups the same way, for the same reason.

Above 720 px nothing changes: left-aligned, subgrid-aligned baselines, exactly as 0015 specified.

## 4. Decision — chart controls

The controls stay **fully configurable** — nothing is removed, and no control becomes
desktop-only. They are re-laid-out by priority:

- **Range presets**: one **horizontally scrollable row** on a phone (they wrap as before from
  `md` up). Twelve chips on one 44 px line instead of three lines; the active chip is the one you
  reach for, and the rest are a thumb-flick away.
- **Navigator** (`‹ − + › ⟲`): always visible, 44 px touch targets, on its own line.
- **Everything else** (smoothing, jump-to-date) folds behind a **`⚙ Options`** disclosure on a
  phone only. On `md` and up the button is hidden and the group is inline — the desktop bar is a
  superset of the phone one, not a different UI.
- The `drag to reorder · eye to hide` hint is desktop-only text (neither gesture is the phone
  interaction — the band drag is, and it advertises itself); the **hidden-panel chips stay visible
  at every width**, because a hidden panel you can't find is a bug.

**Panel heights shrink below 560 px** (124 → 100 px, glyph rows 56 → 46, glued 70 → 58). The stack
loses ~180 px on a phone with no loss of legible amplitude, and heights re-derive on resize.

## 5. Decision — the readout

### Layout

Each reading becomes a **row**, not a phrase: `icon · LABEL` left, value **right-aligned**, on a
hairline baseline. In a 2/3-column grid every value therefore lands on the same right edge in its
column and a wrapped label can no longer shift it. Labels are one line (`truncate`).

The card is **grouped by realm** with a `Sea` / `Air` sub-heading carrying the realm bar + colour,
because the two temperatures and the two directions are the readings most often confused, and a
group heading separates them at a glance where a colour alone did not.

### Sync

**Every panel drives the card**, including tide and Air. Panels on their own x-grid map their
cursor to a **time** (`posToVal`) and then to the nearest buoy index — the same snap the floating
time tag already used — so one card and every crosshair always report one instant. Renders are
deduped by index, so a synced move writes the card once, not once per panel.

### The pinned touch bar (0016 §2) gains values

0016 gave the bar the timestamp only, arguing the on-plot bubbles carry the values. They carry
*one* value, for the panel under your finger. The bar now carries the timestamp **and** a compact
icon+value strip for the core readings (height, period, swell direction, sea temp, wind, wind
direction, air temp) in one horizontally-scrollable line — still ~2 lines tall, still dismissed
only by the ✕.

## 6. Also fixed here

- **The history ribbon was a scroll trap** — `touch-none` on the 48 px track meant a finger
  landing on it could not scroll the page. It now uses the 0016 model: `touch-action: pan-y` plus
  a JS axis-lock, so a vertical swipe scrolls and a horizontal drag still selects a window
  (pointer capture is taken only once the gesture commits to horizontal).
- Navigator buttons were 28 px wide with a 44 px height on touch; they are now square at 44 px.

## 7. Consequences

- 0015's "left-aligned at every width" is now "left-aligned from 720 px up". The `Metric` tile is
  unchanged structurally — subgrid, `cqw` type sizing and the `max-w` ban all still hold, and the
  `clamp` bounds still come from the widest string (Air's `1 020 hPa`) — only the alignment axis
  is breakpoint-dependent.
- 0016's "the bar carries only the timestamp" is superseded by §5.
- `--ts-pad` is now load-bearing: anything positioned against the chart host's padding must read
  it rather than hard-code `1rem`.
